# Task 2.9 — API versioning

## Objective

A versioned prefix every product endpoint hangs from, and probes that stay off it.

## Context

`settings.api_prefix` was added in 2.2 and has been unused since. Health endpoints
(2.6) must not move when the version changes.

## Implementation steps

1. `api/v1/` package with a single `api_router`; a future v2 is a sibling.
2. Mount it under `settings.api_prefix`.
3. Validate the prefix — it is concatenated into every route path.
4. Tests: prefix reachable, bare path not, health unversioned, malformed prefix
   refused.

## Acceptance criteria

- A route on the v1 router answers under the prefix and 404s at the bare path.
- Changing `API_PREFIX` moves every versioned route.
- `/health/live` and `/health/ready` are not under the prefix.
- A prefix without a leading slash, with a trailing slash, uppercase or containing
  a space is refused at startup.
- `scripts/check.sh` passes.

## Tests

Unit, by request.

### Deviations
- **URL prefix, not a header.** Visible in a log line, a bug report and an address
  bar, which is worth more here than header purity.
- **The health check is asserted by request, not by reading `app.routes`.**
  FastAPI 0.141 keeps an included router as one `_IncludedRouter` entry rather
  than flattening its routes, so introspection sees nothing. A behavioural
  assertion is the right one anyway.

### Known limitations
- The v1 router is empty; endpoints arrive with their phases.
- No deprecation mechanism for retiring a version. Nothing to retire yet.
